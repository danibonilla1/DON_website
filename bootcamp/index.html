The Curriculum

Four interconnected modules. From human psychology to artificial intelligence, structured to deliver a portfolio-ready digital asset.

Week 01

The Architecture of Retention

Why do people actually watch — and why do they leave?

Before making content, students must understand attention. We deconstruct the mechanics of digital viewership, moving away from relying on "virality" and toward building sustainable, intentional engagement.

  • The psychology of the first 30 seconds
  • Hook architecture and visual pattern interrupts
  • Decoding YouTube Analytics as a producer
  • Data Case Studies: Content that scales vs. content that dies

Learning Outcomes

  • → Analyze retention curves and identify at least 3 structural weaknesses in a given video
  • → Apply hook architecture principles to construct an opening sequence that sustains the first 30 seconds
  • → Interpret YouTube Analytics data to inform evidence-based content decisions
Week 02

Visual Storytelling Realities

Translating raw footage into emotional impact.

Good ideas die with poor execution. This module adapts traditional cinematic principles for the fast-paced, high-density environment of digital platforms.

  • Adapting the 3-act structure for digital feeds
  • Pacing and silence: Documentary techniques for web
  • Thumbnail psychology as the first narrative frame
  • Live Workshop: Re-editing sequences for maximum retention

Learning Outcomes

  • → Restructure a raw sequence into a 3-act narrative arc optimized for digital attention spans
  • → Design thumbnails applying visual hierarchy and psychological engagement principles
  • → Re-edit a 60-second sequence demonstrating measurable improvement in pacing and emotional arc
Week 03

The AI Digital Workflow

Practical generative AI integrated into real production.

Moving past the hype. We treat Generative AI as a department in the production pipeline, teaching students how to direct models with precision rather than taking whatever the machine outputs. Over 91% of professional creators now use generative AI in production (2025 industry data).

  • Sora 2 and Kling 3.0: Capabilities, limitations & prompting
  • Midjourney & Veo 3.1 for visual asset development
  • AI as an assistant: Style transfer and motion control
  • Live Workshop: Producing mixed-media AI assets

Learning Outcomes

  • → Produce one AI-assisted visual asset using at least two generative tools (image + video)
  • → Evaluate the capabilities and limitations of current AI generation models for production use
  • → Direct AI models with precision prompting, style references, and iterative refinement workflows
Week 04

Creator Business Mechanics

Surviving the market without selling your soul.

Art requires capital to survive. We cover the unglamorous reality of independent digital media, focusing on long-term sustainability over short-term burnouts.

  • Diversified revenue models beyond standard AdSense
  • Audience vs. Business: The critical distinction
  • Ethics, boundaries, and mental health in the Creator Economy
  • Final Showcase: Students present their optimized projects

Learning Outcomes

  • → Design a diversified monetization strategy for a digital content channel
  • → Critically assess the ethical implications and mental health risks of the Creator Economy
  • → Present a complete, portfolio-ready digital content piece with documented strategic rationale

Student Deliverable

Each student produces or optimizes one complete digital video piece across the 4-week program — applying retention architecture, visual storytelling, AI-assisted production, and monetization strategy. This video becomes a portfolio-ready piece they can use professionally.

Assessment Rubric

The final showcase is evaluated on four criteria (0-100 scale, adaptable to university grading systems):

Retention Strategy (25%)

Evidence of intentional hook architecture and pacing decisions throughout the video.

Narrative Structure (25%)

Clear emotional arc, storytelling coherence, and appropriate use of cinematic techniques.

AI Integration (25%)

Purposeful use of at least one generative AI tool as part of the production pipeline.

Strategic Rationale (25%)

Articulated distribution and monetization strategy. The student must defend their creative decisions.

Certification

Students who complete all four modules and the final showcase receive a Certificate of Completion. Grading structure (pass/fail vs. letter grade) can be adapted to your department's requirements.
